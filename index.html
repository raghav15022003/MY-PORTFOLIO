I’m a BCA graduate and Digital Marketing Executive at Magadh Professional Institute, where I’ve been working since December 2024. My role brings together creative communication and practical digital work: managing the institute’s website and social channels, creating content for courses, admissions and events, and supporting campaigns that help students discover the institute.

Alongside social media and promotional design, I update website content, edit videos and coordinate online communication for college activities. My web development background—including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL—also helps me contribute to responsive, database-driven projects and understand how the website experience supports the wider marketing effort.

I enjoy working across disciplines, learning new tools and collaborating with people to turn ideas into useful digital experiences. I bring a creative approach, clear communication and an organised, problem-solving mindset to both independent tasks and team projects.
